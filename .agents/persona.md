# User Persona & Core Design Philosophy

## Design Aesthetic & Standards
- **Anti-AI-Slop Philosophy (Strict & Permanent):** The user strictly rejects synthetic, cheesy, or AI-generated design clichés.
- **Forbidden Elements:**
  - NO neon colors (hyper-saturated green, neon amber, electric cyan).
  - NO glowing box-shadows or blurred colored light flares (`blur-3xl`, multi-color radial orbs).
  - NO pulsing radar beacons or distracting ping animations (`animate-ping`).
  - NO artificial background dot-matrix textures, tech grids, or cyber patterns.
  - NO artificial text shadows.
- **Preferred Aesthetic:**
  - Clean, natural, editorial, and human design.
  - Authentic organic color palettes (e.g., deep botanical forest green `#123428`, warm stone `#f5f5f4`, slate, crisp white).
  - High-contrast, elegant serif and sans-serif typography.
  - Restrained badges: clean 1px borders, subtle translucent fills, or crisp solid white cards with understated status chips.
